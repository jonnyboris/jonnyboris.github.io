Slate Conformer

Matches short clips to a live H.264 stream's spec for seamless slate failover. Everything runs locally with WebCodecs, so nothing is uploaded.

1 Source clip

The short clip or still image to conform. MP4, MOV, WebM, MKV and TS are supported, and so are PNG and JPG stills, which become a video of the output duration.

Drop source clip or click to browse

2 Reference stream optional

Load a sample of the in-flight stream: an HLS URL (master or media playlist), an MP4 or TS URL, or a local segment or file. Its parameters can then be copied into the target spec.
